隱私權政策

最後更新日期：2026 年 9 月 6 日

簡要說明：浮詞沒有自己的帳號系統，也沒有任何屬於我們的伺服器——您的資料不會被傳送到我們這裡，因為我們沒有可以接收它的地方。App 需要連結 Spotify 或 Apple Music（二選一，可隨時更改）才能知道正在播放什麼歌，並會向第三方歌詞資料庫查詢歌詞。App 內含第三方廣告（Google AdMob），詳見第七節。

一、資料收集

浮詞 不收集任何個人資料，也沒有自己的帳號系統：我們不會要求您註冊、不會索取姓名或電子郵件，也沒有任何使用者資料庫。

我們沒有任何伺服器。App 的所有網路連線都是直接連往下列第三方服務，沒有任何資料會經過或儲存在我們手上。

二、連結音樂 App

浮詞需要知道目前播放中的歌曲，才能顯示對應歌詞。您可以選擇 Spotify 或 Apple Music，兩者只會啟用其中一個，並可隨時在設定中更改。

選擇 Apple Music 時

  • App 會請求 iOS 的「媒體與 Apple Music」權限，用來讀取內建「音樂」App 目前正在播放的歌曲資訊（歌名、歌手、專輯、長度、播放進度、隨機與重複播放狀態），以及控制目前的播放
  • 不需要登入，沒有帳號、沒有密碼、沒有任何憑證需要儲存
  • 這些讀取完全在您的裝置上進行，不會傳送到我們或任何第三方（除了第三節所述、為查詢歌詞而送出的歌名與歌手）
  • App 不會瀏覽、匯出或上傳您的音樂收藏，也不會新增、刪除或修改您資料庫中的任何內容
  • 您可以隨時到 iOS 的「設定 → 浮詞」關閉這個權限

選擇 Spotify 時

  • 登入是透過 Spotify 官方的授權流程進行，密碼由 Spotify 自己的頁面處理，本 App 全程看不到也不會保存您的 Spotify 密碼
  • App 取得的權限僅限於：讀取目前播放中的歌曲資訊（歌名、歌手、專輯、長度、播放進度、隨機與重複播放狀態），以及控制目前的播放（播放／暫停、上一首／下一首、跳到指定時間、切換隨機與重複模式）
  • App 無法讀取您的播放清單、音樂收藏或個人檔案，也無法修改您的播放清單或收藏
  • 用於維持登入狀態的憑證儲存在 iOS 鑰匙圈（Keychain），且設定為僅限本機、不同步到 iCloud；更換裝置時需要重新登入

三、歌詞來源

為了取得歌詞，App 會向第三方歌詞資料庫查詢：LRCLIB 與網易雲音樂的公開歌詞服務。

  • 查詢時送出的內容僅有歌曲名稱、歌手、專輯與歌曲長度，用來比對出正確的歌詞
  • 不會送出任何可識別您身分的資訊——沒有帳號、沒有裝置識別碼，這些服務無法得知是誰在查詢
  • 這些是第三方服務，本 App 與其並無合作關係，其可用性與內容不在我們的控制範圍內

四、翻譯與讀音標注

  • 中文翻譯優先使用歌詞來源本身附帶的翻譯；若沒有，日文歌會改用 Apple 內建的裝置端翻譯，處理過程完全在您的裝置上完成，不會上傳到任何地方
  • 平假名與羅馬拼音同樣優先使用歌詞來源附帶的資料，其餘由裝置端自行推算

五、剪貼簿與第三方 AI App

App 提供「複製歌詞給 AI 翻譯」與「用 AI 學這句文法」功能。這些功能只有在您主動點擊時才會執行：將歌詞與提示詞複製到剪貼簿，然後開啟您在設定中選擇的 AI App（Claude、ChatGPT 或 Gemini）。

複製的內容不會傳送給我們。您將其貼入哪個 AI App、之後如何被處理，適用該 App 自身的隱私權政策。

六、本機資料儲存

以下資料只儲存在您的裝置上：

  • Spotify 登入憑證：存於 iOS 鑰匙圈，僅限本機
  • 歌詞快取：已查詢過的歌詞會暫存於裝置，用於加快下次顯示速度
  • 偏好設定：主題顏色、字體大小、歌詞時間校正、假名／羅馬拼音選擇、AI 提示詞等，皆為不具識別性的設定值

刪除 App 即會一併移除上述所有本機資料。

七、第三方服務與廣告

浮詞使用 Google AdMob 顯示廣告（橫幅廣告、原生廣告與獎勵廣告），這是本 App 的收益來源。

  • 浮詞 未實作 App 追蹤透明度（App Tracking Transparency）授權請求，因此不會存取廣告識別碼（IDFA）進行跨 App 追蹤，AdMob 僅會投放非個人化廣告
  • 廣告服務由 Google AdMob 提供，Google 可能依其自身隱私權政策蒐集裝置與技術性資訊以投放廣告，詳情請參閱 Google 隱私權政策
  • 觀看獎勵廣告可解鎖主題顏色並消除當日所有廣告，此狀態僅記錄於您的裝置，於每日午夜重置
  • 除 AdMob 廣告 SDK 外，浮詞 不內建任何分析工具（如 Firebase Analytics、Google Analytics 等），也不使用追蹤像素或 Cookie

八、兒童隱私

浮詞並非針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，我們不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

In brief: 浮詞 has no account system of its own and no servers of ours — your data is never sent to us, because there is nowhere for it to go. The app connects to either Spotify or Apple Music (one at a time, changeable at any time) to know what is playing, and queries third-party lyrics databases for the words. It displays third-party ads (Google AdMob); see Section 7.

1. Data Collection

浮詞 collects no personal data and has no account system of its own: no sign-up, no name or email, no user database.

We operate no servers. Every network request the app makes goes directly to the third-party services listed below; nothing passes through or is stored by us.

2. Connecting to a Music App

浮詞 needs to know which track is playing in order to show the matching lyrics. You choose either Spotify or Apple Music; only one is ever active, and the choice can be changed at any time in Settings.

If you choose Apple Music

  • The app requests the iOS Media & Apple Music permission, used to read what the built-in Music app is currently playing (title, artist, album, duration, playback position, shuffle and repeat state) and to control that playback
  • No sign-in is required — no account, no password, no credential to store
  • These reads happen entirely on your device and are not sent to us or to any third party, apart from the title and artist sent to look up lyrics as described in Section 3
  • The app does not browse, export or upload your music library, and does not add, delete or modify anything in it
  • You can revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings → 浮詞

If you choose Spotify

  • Sign-in uses Spotify's own official authorization flow. Your password is handled by Spotify's own page — this app never sees or stores it
  • The permissions granted are limited to reading the currently playing track (title, artist, album, duration, playback position, shuffle and repeat state) and controlling that playback (play/pause, previous/next, seek, shuffle and repeat)
  • The app cannot read your playlists, saved library, or profile, and cannot modify them
  • The credential that keeps you signed in is stored in the iOS Keychain, marked device-only and not synced to iCloud. Signing in again is required on a new device

3. Lyrics Sources

To find lyrics, the app queries third-party lyrics databases: the public services of LRCLIB and NetEase Cloud Music.

  • A query contains only the track title, artist, album and duration, used to match the correct lyrics
  • No identifying information is sent — no account, no device identifier. These services cannot tell who is asking
  • These are third-party services with which this app has no partnership; their availability and content are outside our control

4. Translation and Reading Aids

  • Translations come first from the lyrics source itself. Where none exist, Japanese songs fall back to Apple's built-in on-device translation, which runs entirely on your device and is never uploaded
  • Hiragana and romaji readings likewise come from the lyrics source where available, and are otherwise derived on the device

5. Clipboard and Third-Party AI Apps

The app can copy lyrics with a prompt for an AI assistant, either for a whole song or for a single line's grammar. These run only when you tap them: the text is copied to the clipboard and the AI app you chose in Settings (Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini) is opened.

The copied text is never sent to us. Whatever you then paste into an AI app is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

6. On-Device Storage

The following is stored only on your device:

  • Spotify credential — in the iOS Keychain, device-only
  • Lyrics cache — fetched lyrics are kept locally so they display faster next time
  • Preferences — theme, font size, lyric timing offset, reading style, AI prompts. None of these identify you

Deleting the app removes all of it.

7. Third-Party Services and Advertising

浮詞 uses Google AdMob to display ads (banner, native and rewarded), which is how the app is funded.

  • 浮詞 does not implement an App Tracking Transparency prompt, so it never accesses the advertising identifier (IDFA) for cross-app tracking, and AdMob serves non-personalized ads only
  • Ads are delivered by Google AdMob, which may collect device and technical information under its own policy — see the Google Privacy Policy
  • Watching a rewarded ad unlocks the theme colours and removes all ads for the rest of the day. That state is recorded on your device only and resets at midnight
  • Apart from the AdMob SDK, 浮詞 includes no analytics tools (no Firebase Analytics, no Google Analytics) and uses no tracking pixels or cookies

8. Children's Privacy

浮詞 is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.
